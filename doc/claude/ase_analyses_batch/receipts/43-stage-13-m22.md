## ⚠ THE HEADLINES

### 1. The planner raises the card's estimate by the noise, through the form's own proc

`ase::ckpt_plan` asks the adapter's `salvage` hook (`tran_points`, the card's count) exactly as before,
then hands that answer to **`ase::stimuli_raise_points`** — the body `ase::stimuli_points` was already

The raised number decides the arming (against `ase::ckpt_floor`), sets the interval (`points / (N+1)`)
and is what the `ASE-CKPT-ARMED` echo and the salvage sentence quote. **The loop in `render_deck` is
untouched** — no line of `render_deck` is in the diff.

### 2. ⚠ THE HOOK STAYS THE CARD'S — S44's shape is the wrong fix, and that is measured

Receipt 41 pinned the old behaviour with NX2's fourth term (`analysis_point_estimate` → 1000 for a noisy
row) and named S44 — *"the salvage estimator made noise-aware"* — as the mutation that reds it. **Making
the hook itself noise-aware is not this fix.** Probed with exactly that hook swapped in (`meas/p2.tcl`),
on a noise row asking 5e8 points:

| | noise check's own caution | §7g `points_max` |
|---|---|---|
| shipped hook (card's count) | `{0 caution}` — *"the noise timestep makes this transient about 5e+08 points …"* / *"use a larger noise timestep, or a shorter stop time"* | silent |
| noise-aware hook | **silent** | **fires** — *"… raise the time step, or lower the stop time"* |

So a noise-aware hook trades the right advice for the wrong one: it tells the user to change the TIME
step for a count the NOISE timestep set. The campaign's **M10 re-runs S44 verbatim and reds NX2, NP1,
NP2 and NP5**. NP5 goes red because a hook reading ngspice's contract hands its noise count to a backend
that declares no contract. **NX2's fourth term does not move; its comment does.**

## What binds later stages

1. **An estimate with more than one reader is one body.** The form's `≈ N points` and the checkpoint plan
   are both `ase::stimuli_raise_points`. A later analysis that adds a stimulus-like table extends that
   proc, never a copy in the planner.
2. **Raise in the planner, not the hook.** An adapter's `salvage` hook answers for the CARD, and three
   readers need exactly that: §7g `points_max`, the noise check's caution and `stimuli_points`.
3. **A noise row judges noise time-weighted** (`integ(v*v)` over time, or `meas … rms`), and absence by a
   peak under a tolerance — never `stddev` over rawfile points.
4. **An `alter` between `stop` and `resume` is unmeasured on 45.2.** A future loop that re-alters mid-run
   measures it on the fork first.
5. **`string is integer -strict` is not "is a number" on Tcl 8.6** — it rejected 1e13 here. A point count
   that can be astronomically large is read with `string is double` or `string is entier` (headline 4).
