## What surprised me — read this first

### 1. The dangerous direction was empty; the *citation* layer is what is rotten

**Zero `STALE-OPEN`** and **zero `UNKNOWN`** in my ten. Every file's headline claim about
whether the defect is live was either right or (once) conservatively stale. What is broken
at scale is the coordinates: **8 of 10 cite at least one file/line/symbol that does not say
what they claim**, and in four of those *every* line number is wrong. That is a different
disease from the one `CREW_BRIEF.md` leads with, and it points stage B somewhere specific.

Worked examples, all read at `8608c7ef`:

* **0654** — 5 cites, 5 wrong. `xschem.tcl:15573` (claimed the `label … statusbar.12`) is a
  **blank line**; the label is at `:18748`. `:14479` (claimed the pack) is
  `set new_sym_ref …`; the pack is at `:16854`. `hilight.c:2201` (claimed `*BUSY*`) is `}`;
  it is at `:2388`. `hilight.c:2305` (claimed the clear) is `break;`; it is at `:2492`.
* **0674** — `ase.tcl:692` and `:802` are the two load-bearing cites; the call site is now
  `:15804` and the catch `:16348`. Drift of ~15 000 lines.
* **0618** — roughly twenty cites in a section literally titled *"What was verified in the
  tree (so a later reader need not re-derive it)"*. `ase.tcl:583-592` (claimed `run_done`)
  is a comment about bus expressions; `run_done` is at `:17629`. `ase_window.tcl:4936-4942`
  (claimed `run_started`) → `:12337`. `test_ase_window.tcl:2229-2233` (claimed W6) is
  **W3e**; W6 is at `:2961-2971`. The section whose stated purpose was to save the next
  reader the re-derivation is the section that cost me the most re-derivation.

**The substance survived the rot every time.** In all eight cases the named symbol still
existed and still behaved as described — only the coordinates died. So a header convention
that records **a tree state** (B1's candidate field) is worth more here than one that
records claim/status, because status was rarely the thing that was wrong.

tracker. Note also that one file has already learned the lesson and says so:
`op_annot.tcl:2366-2368` — *"Cited by function name, not by line number, on purpose: the
line numbers this paragraph used to carry moved by about eleven hundred lines and silently
sent every later reader to the wrong place."* That is the convention B1 should probably
adopt wholesale: **cite by symbol, and only then by line.**

it, because the file's status was accurate when written. B1 should consider that a
