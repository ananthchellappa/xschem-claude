## Does the detector's 4.3% hold up? — **No. Four of seven are false positives.**

| issue | claim is real? | why not |
|---|---|---|
| 0249 | **YES** | genuine: fixed under 0366/0251's mechanism, header never flipped |
| 0216 | **scoped** | claim says *"for this path"*; the ASE path is explicitly out of scope |
| 0650 | **scoped** | the claim is a *plan* in 0653; the work landed, but only half of it |
| 0071 | **NO** | `CLOSED … (issue 0071 atom 6)` closes **0003**; 0071 is credited, not closed |
| 0264 | **NO ×3** | *"filed, **not fixed** … issue 0264"* — negation, and it is 0362/0363's own status |
| 0516 | **NO ×2** | *"FILED, **not** closed: issue 0516"* / *"FILED, **not silently fixed**"* |
| 0947 | **NO** | *"…and fixes 0947 at the same time"* — 0946's unimplemented **option 3** |

**Three mechanical false-positive shapes, all fixable, none requiring judgement:**

1. **Negation** (0264 ×3, 0516 ×2). `CLOSER`'s `[^.\n]{0,40}?` gap spans `, not `,
   `, filed, not `, `, not silently `. **Fix:** reject when the 40-char gap, or the ~25
   chars immediately *preceding* the verb, contains `not|never|n't|cannot|un-|without`.
   Cheap, and it removes 5 of the 8 bad claims outright.
2. **Attribution, not closure** (0071 ×6). A child's status line credits the umbrella:
   `**Status:** CLOSED 2026-07-14 (issue 0071 atom 6)`. **Fix:** when the number appears
   **in parentheses** directly after a date or immediately following a status verb at the
   head of a *different* issue file, it is provenance. A narrower rule that works today:
   `(issue N atom M)` is never a closure of N.
3. **Prescription, not record** (0947 ×1, 0650 ×2). *"that closes 0650"*, *"…and fixes
   0947 at the same time"* — future/conditional, and in 0947's case inside a numbered
   **Options** list. **Fix:** suppress matches inside a section whose heading matches
   `Options|Suggested|What to do|Next|Candidate`, and treat `that closes N` / `would
   close N` (verb preceded by `that|which|would|will|to`) as a proposal.

**And the class the detector was built to find is far rarer than 4.3%.** A4 defined it as
*filed as A → fixed later under B → B's header names A → A never updated*. In this
flagged set that is **one file, 0249** — and even 0249 is the milder variant, because its
resolution is inside its own body rather than only in B. **1 of 165 = 0.6%**, plus two
partials. The **4.3% is the rate of "a header says open while some sentence somewhere put
a closure verb near its number"**, which is a much noisier quantity. Quote it as that, or
not at all.

**1438 IS flagged** — a true positive — and `sys.exit(1)`s otherwise. That is the right
rule half-applied: it proves the scanner is not blind, and proves nothing about whether it
