## 1. The dispatch brief's central measurement is wrong, and it is wrong in an instructive way

**The brief says:** *"54 of the 71 were filed on a single day, 2026-09-10, then 1, 1, 10, 1,
3, 1 across the following week."*

**Measured — the driver read file MTIMES, not filing dates.**

```sh
for f in ~/.claude/xschem_owed/look/*; do date -r "$f" +%Y-%m-%d; done | sort | uniq -c
#  -> 54 2026-09-10 · 1 · 1 · 10 · 1 · 3 · 1      EXACTLY the brief's numbers

for f in ~/.claude/xschem_owed/look/*; do date -d @$(head -1 "$f" | cut -f1) +%Y-%m-%d; done | sort | uniq -c
#  -> 1 09-02 · 1 09-03 · 4 09-04 · 25 09-05 · 4 09-06 · 10 09-08 · 3 09-09 · 6 09-10
#     · 1 09-11 · 1 09-12 · 10 09-13 · 1 09-14 · 3 09-15 · 1 09-16
```

The tail matches because those files have not been touched since. The head does not: the
brief's **54** is `1+1+4+25+4+10+3+6` — **eight days summed and attributed to the last of
them**.

**The smoking gun is the burst:** of the 54, **53 share the identical mtime
`2026-09-10 12:18:38`**, and the 54th is `13:25:32`. Fifty-three files do not get filed in the
same second — that is the `repo:` stamping pass rewriting every entry in place, and
`CLAUDE.md` independently measures the ledger **fully stamped at 12:40**, twenty-two minutes
later.

The straggler is confirmation rather than an exception. The single `13:25:32` file is
`hier_pdf_nav_1357_H6` — **the one entry of the 71 that carries no `repo:` stamp** — and
`CLAUDE.md` already names that exact file at that exact second as one of *"one `owed.sh` run
filing three debts"*. So the one look entry the stamping pass did not rewrite is the one whose
mtime is not in the burst, which is what a migration looks like from the outside.

**What it costs:** the real peak is **25 on 2026-09-05**, and **2026-09-10 has 6**. So the
shape argument survives — the debts really are bunched — but two other claims in the same
paragraph do not:

* *"one batch discharging its pixel debts"* — it is **three**, on two clones: the Results-window
  work (35 entries, 09-02→09-06), the ASE-L analyses work (17, 09-11→09-16), and a PDF batch
  in `~/dev/xschem-op-wcard` (11, 09-08→09-10).
* *"the identical defect E2 just collapsed"* — the shape rhymes, the ratio does not. E2's 48
  were **one decision asked 48 times** (48 → 16, and 36 of them already in one document).
  These are **genuinely different screens**: 71 → 27, and the compression comes from grouping
  by surface, not from finding one repeated question.

⚠ **The mtime trap is this batch's own subject wearing new clothes.** A file's mtime is a
*proxy* for when it was written; the timestamp inside it is the thing. `CLAUDE.md` records
getting cases-vs-lines wrong three times, E1 records a fourth, E2 a fifth. **This is the
sixth, and it is the first one where the proxy and the thing disagreed by an entire batch.**
